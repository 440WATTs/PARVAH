# Official Data Sources & Government Welfare Registry

All welfare scheme records, emergency contact protocols, and entitlements displayed in **Pahad Saathi** are sourced from verified official portals of the Government of Sikkim and the Government of India. 

*Last Verified Date for all entries: October 15, 2026.*

## 1. Verified Elderly Welfare & Pension Schemes

### A. Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)
- **Administering Authority**: Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India & Social Welfare Department, Government of Sikkim.
- **Description**: Central assistance pension for seniors aged 60 and above living below the poverty line.
- **Official Source Portal**: [National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)](https://nsap.nic.in/)
- **Eligibility**: Age 60+, Below Poverty Line (BPL) cardholder, Sikkim Subject / Certificate of Identification (COI).
- **Benefits**: ₹1,000 to ₹1,500 monthly Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to bank account.

### B. Sikkim Mukhya Mantri Vridha Samman Pension (State Old Age Pension)
- **Administering Authority**: Social Welfare Department, Government of Sikkim, Tashiling Secretariat, Gangtok.
- **Description**: State government social security grant for elderly residents not fully accommodated under NSAP central quotas.
- **Official Source Portal**: [Government of Sikkim Social Welfare Department](https://sikkim.gov.in/departments/social-welfare-department)
- **Eligibility**: Age 65+, permanent resident of Sikkim, Gram Panchayat recommendation, not receiving other state/central government pensions.
- **Benefits**: Monthly state pension credited via Gram Panchayat Unit (GPU) / Bank DBT.

### C. Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY & Sikkim Mukhya Mantri Swasthya Bima Yojana
- **Administering Authority**: State Health Agency (SHA), Health & Family Welfare Department, Government of Sikkim.
- **Description**: Comprehensive secondary and tertiary hospitalization cover for senior citizens and low-income families.
- **Official Source Portal**: [National Health Authority (PM-JAY)](https://nha.gov.in/PM-JAY)
- **Empanelled Facilities in Sikkim**: STNM Multi-Speciality Hospital (Sichey, Gangtok), Gyalshing District Hospital, Namchi District Hospital, Central Referral Hospital (Manipal Tadong).
- **Benefits**: Up to ₹5,00,000 per family per year cashless treatment with priority senior citizen registration.

### D. Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY) — Assistive Physical Devices
- **Administering Authority**: Artificial Limbs Manufacturing Corporation of India (ALIMCO) & Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India.
- **Description**: Physical distribution of assisted-living devices for low-income senior citizens suffering from age-related physical limitations.
- **Official Source Portal**: [ALIMCO Government Portal](https://alimco.in/page.aspx?id=12)
- **Benefits**: Free walking sticks, hearing aids, wheelchairs, tripod canes, and spectacles provided via District Medical Assessment Camps.

### E. National Elderline 14567 (Sikkim Chapter)
- **Administering Authority**: National Institute of Social Defence (NISD), Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
- **Description**: Toll-free tele-helpline for senior citizens providing emotional support, rescue in abandonment, and legal guidance under the Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act.
- **Official Source Portal**: [Elderline National Portal](https://elderline.dosje.gov.in/)
- **Toll-Free Helpline**: **14567** (Operating 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM all 7 days).

## 2. Emergency Telephony Numbers in India

- **112**: Single Unified National Emergency Response Number (Police, Fire, Ambulance).
- **108**: National Emergency Medical Transport & Ambulance Services (Sikkim Chapter).
- **1090**: Women & Elderly Distress Helpline.
- **14567**: Senior Citizen National Elderline.
